#  My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man

## About the Movie

**Spider-Man: No Way Home** is one of my favorite superhero movies. It is an action and adventure movie about Peter Parker, who faces challenges while protecting the people he loves.

##  Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because:
- It has exciting action scenes.
- It teaches the importance of responsibility.
- It shows the value of friendship and family.
- It has an interesting and emotional story.

##  My Favorite Characters

1. Peter Parker
2. MJ
3. Ned Leeds
4. Doctor Strange
5. Aunt May
